## §5.4 revamp evaluation

**Verdict: no revamp warranted at this gate.** Evidence: a 5 s sample
profile on the n=260 reference shows propagate at 81% of samples,
analyze ~15%, and the assignment path at 0.6% — the kissat-like shape
the phase-1 kernel was built for. The one structural rewrite this phase
(per-literal watch vectors → flat watch arena) was executed under the
revamp discipline and is performing as designed (2.4× on the reference
instance at the time). Two hot-path hypotheses were tested and honestly
rejected this week: header-inlining the assignment path (~0%) and LTO
(~0%) — per-conflict cost is memory-bound watch scanning, not call
overhead. No rule needed contortions to meet the §5.3 contract
(shrinking landed rule-local; OTFS needed a deliberate, recorded
deviation — allocate-new instead of in-place shrink — precisely so it
would NOT need kernel-wide shrunken-clause machinery). Fuzz/invariant
findings are not clustering on any design decision. Next structural
candidate if the T2 propagation microbenchmark misses: watch-arena
cache-line packing, an incremental layout change, not a rewrite.
