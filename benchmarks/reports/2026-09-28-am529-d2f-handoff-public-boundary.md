# AM529: bounded public boundary of the two direct-Proof-API D2F handoffs

The two archived AM465 D2F handoffs (`4651064600` and `4651234831`) remain real canonical no-option DSR failures when their `S.script` programs call `Proof` directly. AM476 recompiled the driver against the repaired serializer and reproduced the same rejected proof bytes. This report classifies their **exact public reach**, not the entire possible D2F failure family.

Both scripts execute proof operations outside any public rule application, including direct clause/unit deletion (`D`) and proof-only external additions (`E`). The first starting CNF declares 52 variables, but its failing SR proof line has clause `(56 ∨ 12)`, after proof-only extension `E 15`; the second declares 17 variables, but its failing SR clause is `(22 ∨ 5 ∨ 9 ∨ -15)`, after proof-only extension events. A public typed X7 tuple in `tactix_online_configure_v2` converts each external literal through `kernel_internalize` (`src/api/api.cpp`), which returns `INVALID_LIT` when the external identity is absent from `external_to_internal` (`src/kernel/kernel.hpp`). Thus neither failing X7 tuple is a legal public action on its starting CNF. The direct scripts also bypass root-reason retirement and unit publication performed by `kernel_delete_clause`/`kernel_delete_binary` before public-rule deletions (`src/kernel/kernel.hpp`, `src/kernel/kernel.cpp`). These are specific differences between the direct Proof driver and the solver path, not an excuse to alter the checker contract or narrow legal X7 actions.

The public same-CNF Probe/BCE/X7 control in AM476 took a different, completely checked DSR→LSR→Trestle route. AM483's 448,000 public API programs did not reach D2F. Neither observation proves that a different legal public sequence could never create an analogous checker-retained clause. The direct handoffs remain open as a serializer/checker-state limitation; broad public N1/N2 acceptance remains pending. A future general repair must preserve legal X7 actions and the canonical no-option DSR route, or a changed contract must be explicitly chosen by the user.
