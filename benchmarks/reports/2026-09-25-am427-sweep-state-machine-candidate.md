## Candidate state and transitions

`Kernel::sweep_marked` records per-external-variable pass marks, stable through variable compaction; `Kernel::sweep_incomplete` records whether the current pass is unfinished, independently of saved front/tail entries. At entry, Sweep revalidates retained entries once and clears marks for unschedulable entries. If the pass is complete, it admits all fresh eligible seeds after retained ones; if incomplete, only still-marked unscheduled seeds may be added. When no active marks remain at schedule entry, an incomplete old pass completes and a new pass is marked, even if the schedule is empty. Popping a seed clears its mark before processing. At exit, no active marks completes the current pass, possibly leaving an unmarked representative in the saved schedule. The preexisting public pure-seed rule surface and the nonresumable path are unchanged. A full-variable sort is skipped on ordinary resume calls with no unqueued marked seeds; mark scans remain linear, as in the references. The trace memory census includes the new vector.

`test_sweep_retained` now covers both lifecycle branches: a filtered marked tail completes old and new empty passes with no same-call fresh admission; an unmarked retained seed admits a fresh seed; and a productive four-variable formula completes a pass while keeping its previously swept representative, then admits fresh work on the next call. The latter is an executed formula-level state, not only a hand-constructed flag arrangement. The fixture also distinguishes newly admitted from resumed seeds in `sweep_resumed`. Existing retained front/tail, bounds, holes and same-call rescheduling checks remain. Synthetic retained fixtures in `test_sweep`, `test_sweep_small_env` and `test_sweep_retained` explicitly seed the new pass marks. `test_kernel::test_variable_compaction` pins the external mark identities and incomplete flag across internal renumbering.
