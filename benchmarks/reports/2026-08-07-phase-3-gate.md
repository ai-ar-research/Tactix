## 4 — reach

From the Tier B array's own per-task `reached` counters (`20260806-235833-B-shipped`; 24 of 28 tasks
produced stats, the 4 timeouts contributing nothing): **every registry entry fires except `Reorder`**.

Two corrections were needed to trust that number, and both are the same shape — a counter that cannot
move makes a rule that ran look untested:

- Five `dosed` rules were keyed on ROUND counters that `apply_instance` never increments; `9vliw_bp_mc`
  prints `c bve 0 rounds, 11008 eliminated`. Re-keyed to attempt counters.
- `ConflictAnalysis` was keyed on `c conflicts`, which the **Propagate** rule increments — so
  `--require-reached ConflictAnalysis` was satisfiable without ConflictAnalysis running at all. Re-keyed
  to `c learned`.

Both retire the "19 of 25 registry entries fire" figure that decision (98f9bfd) cited; it was measured on fuzz-sized
instances through a broken instrument, and the registry has 26 entries.

Separately, an audit established at the DISPATCH (trace payload zipped with per-step `ApplyResult`, so no
other rule's side effects can move the count) that **all 26 entries are driven under `--random-policy`
and all do work**, over two independent case populations.
