# AM377: malformed X5 online replay literal repair

Date: 2026-09-24. This is an **ungated robustness checkpoint** on top of AM376, not N1/N2 or online-learning acceptance. The AM376 Opus owner continues on its immutable older source; a successor affected-scope owner is required for this repair. No Tier D run was submitted.

## Finding and causal control

The online replay parser accepted any `uint32_t` as a Condition literal. `UINT32_MAX` has no signed-DIMACS spelling: `var_of(UINT32_MAX)` is `INT_MAX`, and the old atomic application called `externalize()` before checking the live mapping, overflowing the signed `var+1` result. An explicit `OnlineStrategyProgram` could carry the same malformed encoding. The new `test_random_online` assertion that validation reject this literal **failed on the AM376 source** (Release `random_online` aborted at the new assertion). This is an invalid-input failure, not evidence of a bad proof or model from a legal tuple.

`validate_online_strategy_program` now checks every Condition clause and cube literal for a representable external DIMACS identity. It does not impose a cube length or formula-size cap. Atomic application remaps the saved external `Lit` directly through `external_to_internal`, without signed conversion; a subsequently retired clause identity declines before publication. The test checks rejection of the invalid encoding in both direct validation and deserialization, and acceptance of the largest valid external variable (`UINT32_MAX-3`, DIMACS `INT_MAX`). Existing v1/v2 and DefaultPolicy paths are unchanged. The abstract X5 legality rule is unchanged; §16 of `docs/implementation-notes.md` records the boundary.
