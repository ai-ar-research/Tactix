# The tick unit, made commensurable with the references

**Date:** 2026-08-07 · **Change:** `src/kernel/kernel.hpp` (`kernel_cache_lines`), `src/kernel/kernel.cpp`
(four scan sites + three event charges) · **Phase 4 §2, first meter defect.**

## 1. Why this had to land first, and alone

Every effort per-mille either reference ships is a **fraction of a tick count charged their way**. Porting
kissat's `sweepeffort` = 100 ‰ onto a tick that counts something else transfers a number that means something
else. So the unit is upstream of the whole dosing port, and because it re-prices every budget in the solver —
and the mode-switch schedule, which is also tick-denominated — it lands by itself with a re-baseline.

## 2. What the references charge, read at the source

`kissat_cache_lines(n, sizeof(watch))` (`kissat/src/utilities.h:21-35`) with
`ASSUMED_LD_CACHE_LINE_BYTES = 7`: a **128-byte** line, 4-byte watches, `shift = 7 − 2 = 5`, and the count
**rounds up** — `(n + 31) >> 5` = `ceil(n/32)`, zero for an empty list.

`propagate_literal` (`kissat/src/proplit.h`) then charges, per literal propagated:

| site | charge |
|---|---|
| `:64` | `1 + cache_lines(size_watches)` — the watch-list scan |
| `:92` | `+1` per **binary assignment** made |
| `:98` | `+1` per **large-clause dereference** |
| `:148` | `+1` per **watch relocation** |
| `:174` | `+1` per **large-clause assignment** |

## 3. What we were charging

`1 + (n >> 4)` — a **64-byte** line, 16 watches per line, and **floored** — plus **one** of the four event
charges (the large-clause dereference). So we were:

- **over-charging the scan** by 2× (n/16 against n/32), and
- **under-charging events** by three of four.

The two do not cancel. Measured, the second dominates.

## 4. The measurement — isolated

28 Tier B instances at a 120 s cap, `pin-d3-hbrcap` (HEAD−1) against `pin-tickunit` (HEAD), counters only
(`benchmarks/probes/tickunit-compare.sh`, job `1589977`). The unit change moves the budgets, which moves the
search, so a raw before/after ratio is a *search* effect on most instances. **The unit effect is only readable
where the search barely diverged**, so the table separates them:

| instance (conflicts within 10 %) | ticks before | ticks after | ratio | conflicts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `hwmcc17miters-xits-iso-oski15a08b00s` | 7,296,376 | 15,798,835 | **2.17** | 1 → 1 |
| `qwh.50.1250.shuffled-as.sat03-1655` | 156,659,792 | 400,046,449 | 2.55 | 46,912 → 45,747 |
| `b22_1` | 1,012,139,896 | 2,097,071,544 | 2.07 | 474,108 → 492,248 |
| `9vliw_bp_mc.shuffled` | 280,406,020 | 493,213,937 | 1.76 | 314,336 → 312,343 |
| `sted5_0x24204-50` | 1,193,032,014 | 1,945,503,896 | 1.63 | 435,919 → 446,889 |
| `x9-08014.sat.sanitized` | 786,297,268 | 1,145,156,333 | 1.46 | 359,340 → 339,815 |

| `clique_n2_k10.sanitized` | 122,683,505 | 154,228,784 | 1.26 | 168,695 → 170,633 |

**Median 1.76×, range 1.26–2.55× over the 8 instances whose search held still.** The cleanest single reading is
`hwmcc17miters`, which takes **exactly one conflict in both runs** — a deterministic prefix, so 2.17× is very
nearly the pure unit ratio there. The spread across instances is expected and is the point: the ratio depends
on how much of a propagation is scanning versus assigning, and that mix is a property of the formula.

**So our ticks were UNDER-charging kissat's by roughly 1.8×**, despite the coarser cache line, because three of
the four event charges were missing.

## 5. The consequence is ASYMMETRIC, and this is the part a naive port would hide

`search_ticks` is the reference every allowance is a per-mille of, and it just grew ~1.76×. Whether a rule's
*real* budget grew depends on whether its **meter** grew with it:

| meter | currency | effect |
|---|---|---|
| Probe, Backbone, Vivify, Instantiate, Sweep, Congruence, HyperBinaryRes, TransitiveReduce | propagation ticks | budget **and** spend both ~1.76× — roughly a wash |
| **BVE** (`bve_resolutions`) | resolutions | **budget ~1.76×, spend unchanged — a real increase** |
| **Strengthen** (`strengthen_visits`), **SubsumeForward** (`forward_visits`) | partner visits | **same — a real increase** |

Those three entries were being **starved by an under-charging tick**. That is consistent with the standing N4
finding — our BVE eliminates zero variables on 7 of the 9 instances where kissat eliminates anything — and it
is the most plausible mechanism behind the Tier B result below.

`pin-tickunit` were verified byte-identical (sha256 `e9d8ccd869ef379f…`). Read the manifest sha as "at least
this", not as the change under test.

## 7. Soundness

`ctest` 58/58. Fuzz on the pinned binary, **0 failures**: DRAT 10,000 cases (job `1589740`) and native LRAT
10,000 (`1589741`), coverage 9,821 / 9,819, non-termination 1.8 % — below the 3.0 % recorded for the previous
DefaultPolicy legs.

## 8. One deliberate divergence, recorded

kissat charges the dereference for a clause it then discards as `ignore`, because it compares *pointers* and
must dereference first (`proplit.h:98` precedes the `c == ignore` test in the probing variant). We compare
`ref == ignore` and never dereference, so we do not charge. Ours is the more accurate memory-traffic proxy and
the difference is one tick per ignored clause, in probing propagation only.

## 9. What this unblocks

The per-mille values in `EFFORT_SPECS` are now fractions of a quantity commensurable with the references'.
Until this landed, **every one of them was a number ported across a unit mismatch** — including the five that
the design pass found are backed by no reference at all. The next increments (§2: the phase anchor, the
unbacked fractions, the taxonomy) can now be measured against a ledger that means what kissat's means.
