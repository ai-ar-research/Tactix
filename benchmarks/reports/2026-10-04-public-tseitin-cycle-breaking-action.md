rebuilds the complete plan, and exact-matches all authenticated state before

the startup certificate boundary. Propagation remains a separate action. At the
next propagated root, a fresh census can expose another component or an
alternative cycle whose edges remain unassigned. Root-fixed off-cycle edges are
allowed and recorded; selected cycle edges must be unassigned.

The current public domain is deliberately conservative. Any unclaimed stored

is satisfied at root. This physical residual boundary is stronger than the
mathematical X7 precondition and must remain visible in future N1 review.

variable. It adds no branchable auxiliary and needs no reconstruction witness;
the original-variable model projection is unchanged. DRAT, LRAT, and PBP

proof IDs, cycle and X7 witness, proof events, installed unit,

Applied, duplicate, and complete no-structure outcomes consume their exact

until context changes. Resource exhaustion and interruption retain the payload

without rediscovery, while formula, session, or owner change discards it. This
settlement rule is shared with the corrected GF(3) and DirectCSP producers. No
