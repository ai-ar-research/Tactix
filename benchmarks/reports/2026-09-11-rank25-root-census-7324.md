Allocator values are mallinfo2 bookkeeping, not object-by-object liveness or
resident bytes; mmap allocations are reported separately. Tcache and allocator
metadata complicate interpretation. Capacities, hole bytes and allocated heap
must not be summed as independent RSS or promised savings. The snapshot is
after the BVE application, not during its temporary old/new-buffer overlap.

The observation strengthens the source-level lead in 7320/7321: Tactix keeps
millions of root-fixed Active slots in its physical arrays, while Kissat folds
fixed variables in physical compaction. Tactix's virtual compact import order
matches scheduling semantics, not actual hot-storage shrinkage. The allocator
figures point away from an entirely free-but-retained heap explanation, but
do not establish each object's contribution to the ordinary peak.

Rank 25 is not simply accumulating memory because it runs longer: the ordinary
full result is 224.225s Tactix versus 246.184s Kissat, with exact-reproduced peaks
2484880 versus 888820 KiB. Search and simplification trajectories differ; this
does not prove every phase has lower work or better unit cost.

## Runtime bookkeeping and next action

The 7185 audit remains applicable. Release uses -O3 -DNDEBUG; heavyweight event
trace, journal, proof shadow and conflict-stop diagnostics are off. Optional
scan counters, individual rule clocks and some environment guards remain.
The 7172 joint ablation did not show a consistent net speedup, which is not
proof of zero overhead. Decompression, hashing, independent checking and
cleanup remain outside solver timing; proof emission and ordinary statistics
remain inside. The new census is NOT enabled in runtime evaluation builds.

Next, confirm the ordinary binary's root populations/capacities externally
at the reconstruction-growth boundary, without inferior allocations, before
using the census as exact peak attribution. Then evaluate hot/cold root-state
separation and dense-storage lifetime as structural mechanisms. Do not tune
the hole threshold from this one sample; its current 75% rule follows native.
Preserve distinct public/proof identities, unit IDs, reasons, model rebuilding
and scheduling/RNG/queue closures. Add tests for any changed mapping/lifetime.

Rank 31 remains the largest unshelved time gap (264.665s): phase-aligned work
is required before comparing CDCL unit cost. Root-storage diagnosis is not a
claim that it alone closes the overall runtime gap. N1--N4, Rank 21 shelving
and the fixed native denominator remain unchanged.
