# LymphoSAT-derived production mechanism overfit audit

## Scope and standard

This is a bounded source-and-evidence audit of the LymphoSAT-derived mechanisms
that can affect ordinary `DefaultPolicy`: private SAT constructors, Ramsey
startup, graph-labeling Window, local-search guidance, and the retired noL
surface.  It does not re-audit generic Kissat-style Walk or the independent
checker repair.  No solver was run and no production setting changed.

The audit distinguishes three questions which existing correctness tests do not
collapse:

1. **Soundness:** a published SAT answer is checked against the entire live CNF,
   and a published UNSAT answer has a checked proof.
2. **Structural applicability:** admission follows an exact mathematical or
   encoding contract rather than a filename, rank, stored witness, or accidental
   dimensions.
3. **Policy value:** varied held-out evidence shows enough causal benefit to pay
   for running the mechanism by default.

Whole-CNF validation answers the first question for a model constructor.  It
does not make a family-specific recognizer or witness search general, and it
does not show net policy benefit.

## Actionable policy boundary

The source contains no filename, benchmark rank, or stored winning assignment in
these active paths.  The larger overfit risk is policy composition: a sequence
of default-on specialist searches, each carrying owner-specific shape and work
constants, can act like an implicit benchmark portfolio even though every
individual result is sound.

The current low-risk boundary is:

1. keep noL retired and keep graph Window opt-in;
2. keep Ramsey's exact n=18/19 proof action, with its scope stated honestly;
3. keep generic authenticated representations, proof-producing actions and the
   guidance transport;
4. preserve Lucky17 while its unusually clean causal/preservation evidence is
   requalified on the intended candidate;
5. require strict-image and varied-cohort evidence before retaining Lucky18 as a
   normal default; and
6. treat Lucky10–14 private witness searches as review/removal candidates from
   ordinary Default startup, retaining explicit public actions until
   constructor-disabled replacements or current causal cohorts decide them.

Any later enable/disable experiment should use a preregistered family cohort
with structurally varied positives, near-miss negatives, model/proof checking,
per-action cost and the four standing regression gates.  Constants should come
from an algorithmic bound or a faithful cited reference profile; target-derived
values stay explicit diagnostic parameters.  A passing correctness suite is a
necessary gate, not evidence of generality or net benefit.
