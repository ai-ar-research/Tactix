receipts count zero registered-rule applications, claim no rule progress, and
count zero application-composition work. Before/after observations still retain
actual changes. Dedicated native meters, their currencies, overshoot and overflow
remain intact. An application-unit support call without a dedicated meter uses
the already captured native tick delta rather than a synthetic application.
Explicit native composition currencies and status/stop outcomes are preserved.
Ten duplicated normalization callbacks were removed; Warmup and SparseCollect
retain their specialized result mapping. Receiver-free execution remains direct.

The preserved default-private arena-ordering scope is now a shared typed
`ArenaOrderingScope`. Installation timing and caller-wire restoration, including
nested null and early terminal scopes, remain unchanged. The ordinary Eliminate
definition-session increment now uses `BeginBveDefinitionSessionRequest`, with a
support receipt describing the old/next epoch. The policy retains its original
ordinary-phase condition; doses do not create sessions and fast elimination does
not inherit the ordinary increment. Permanent material-profile installation now
has a receipt at the original scheduler-build boundary, recording selected versus
inherited flags. Temporary material overrides keep their native restoration.

No owner member layout, native rule implementation, scheduling decision, adaptive
dose, configuration collection, sampler, proof routing or Kernel source changed.
The executable comparisons below support preservation within their declared
fixture scope; they do not establish comprehensive correspondence or performance.
