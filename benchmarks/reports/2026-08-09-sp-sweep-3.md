# SP-SWEEP-3 closure

**Outcome:** implemented and retained, but **ships off**. The schedule is live,
correctness-clean, and separate from the complete per-seed Sweep rule surface;
it loses solved instances on both Tier B and Tier C.

## Mechanism and boundary

Commit `c26b8da` ports Kissat's canonical Sweep schedule: retain the ranked,
both-polarity-eligible untried seed tail across calls, consume a seed before its
attempt, and grow the environment variable, clause, and depth caps only after
true exhaustion. Retained identities are external variable IDs, so kernel
compaction cannot renumber the schedule.

The port changes only canonical scheduling. `sweep_seeds()` and
`sweep_try(seed)` remain complete and unchanged, including access to variables
excluded by the canonical occurrence-cap filter. The shipped default is off;
`TACTIX_SWEEP_RESUME=1` enables the experimental arm.

## Correctness and reach

- Release tests passed 60/60. Focused assertion/address/undefined-sanitizer
  tests passed 8/8, including schedule exhaustion, cap growth, off-arm identity,
  and compaction-stable external keys.
- Harness self-test job `1595358` passed.
- Enabled normal-policy LRAT fuzz job `1595359` ran 10,000 cases with zero
  failures and 9,827 semantic verdicts; `SweepResume` fired in 503 cases.
- Targeted stress job `1595360` had zero semantic failures in 10,000 cases and
  2,675,201 direct Sweep applications, but failed its `SweepResume` reach
  requirement. This is expected boundary evidence: stress policy invokes the
  complete rule instance directly, while continuation belongs to the canonical
  scheduler. It is not counted as a passing continuation-reach leg.
- Probe `1596397` on `b22_1` observed 33,426 scheduled and 416 resumed seeds,
  with a large retained tail. Probe `1596398` on `php-7` observed 56 scheduled,
  51 resumed, one completed pass, and an empty tail. Thus both continuation and
  exhaustion-driven completion are directly witnessed.

All A/B/C result files report `correctness.clean=true`: no verdict mismatch,
invalid model, invalid certificate, checker give-up, or unverified scored arm.

Tier B's two enabled-arm timeouts are
`pyhala-braun-sat-35-4-04.shuffled.cnf.xz` and
`2013113162201nw.shuffled-as.sat03-1668.cnf.xz`. Removing both reverses the

while enabled times out. Two Tier C instances reverse the residual timing sign.
The timing effect is therefore not robust, but the solved-count regression is
repeated across both decision tiers.

## Decision

Keep `sweep_resume=false`. The mechanism is a faithful scheduler option and its
proof-rule boundary is clean, but matching a Kissat scheduling detail is not
sufficient reason to degrade Tactix's current default. This closes SP-SWEEP-3
without reviving the earlier bare cursor. The next ledger item is SP-SWEEP-7,
which is rule work: a full environment-literal partition plus model-flip
prefilter.
