# AM477: CaDiCaL and Kissat proof producers mapped to Tactix actions

This is a read-only producer-source audit on Tactix `d464f5f6`, pinned CaDiCaL `7b99c07f`, SC24 Kissat `1e56df6c`, and AE-Kissat-MAB `da40eb95`. It extends the AM398/403 emitter-interface audit to direct producer call sites and shared constructors; it is not an N1 reachability, N2 certificate, or N3/N4 performance acceptance. Kissat and AE-Kissat-MAB have byte-identical `src/proof.c` (SHA-256 `dd393d37...`).

The source anchors are direct: Kissat `proof.h:21-114` exports only clause/binary/unit/empty additions, shrink, and deletion; `proof.c:317-403` serializes those events. The direct call-site scan of `src/*.[ch]` includes `congruence.c`, `sweep.c`, `substitute.c`, `vivify.c`, `forward.c`, `fastel.c`, and the common `clause.c` constructors. CaDiCaL's `proof.cpp:556-674` dispatches derived-clause, deletion, restoration/strengthening metadata and incremental-query events; `clause.cpp:555-595` sends hyper-resolution as derived clauses and Factor's fresh-variable step as `add_derived_rat_clause`. `block.cpp:270-278`, `condition.cpp:786-797`, `cover.cpp:395-445` and `factor.cpp:605-665` show the other producer-specific event classes. `weaken_plus` is restoration metadata plus deletion (`proof.cpp:412-419`), not an additional inference. CaDiCaL's `veripbtracer.cpp:193-271` renders these nonincremental clause events as `red`, `pol` or PB `rup`; AM403 checked that this serializer does not add a new CaDiCaL producer rule.

The **bounded conclusion** is that these inspected nonincremental CaDiCaL/Kissat producers do not reveal a further proof-event primitive beyond Tactix's declared RUP/RAT/deletion/reconstruction catalog. This is a source-to-event mapping, not proof that Tactix admits every corresponding legal instance or matches every producer's schedule. Common constructors can hide call paths, and no current-source public reach/certificate matrix is supplied here. CaDiCaL assumptions/constraints/conclusions (`assume.cpp`, `external.cpp`, `internal.cpp`) are a distinct incremental protocol deferred by plan O7. The separate AM402 finding remains: active LymphoSAT producers and Satsuma's VeriPB mode use general PB arithmetic/redundancy/dominance, while Tactix's PB path is limited to complete startup refutations. Whether to add a general live PB action needs an explicit architecture and proof-format decision, not a family-specific emitter. N1/N2, the winner performance gates and Tier D remain open.
