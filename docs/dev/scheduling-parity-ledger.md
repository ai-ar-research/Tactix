## 13. The scheduling-parity ledger (evidence for N4 verification)

**Plan decision (f22423c), broadened 2026-09-17, §1.1 N4** requires DefaultPolicy's
order, effort, warm-ups, watermarks, ramps, feedback, and couplings to be **at
least as sophisticated as the state-of-the-art owner of each integrated
technique**, including Kissat, Satsuma, and LymphoSAT in the frozen landscape.
Per owner, this register records an at-least-as-strong analogue or names the gap
and closer; an empty or stale ledger is itself a finding at every phase gate.
**plan decision (7cb3212) makes this ledger a GATE:** N4 verification is complete only when no row reads
`WE_LACK_IT` or `WE_ARE_CRUDER` without closure or an accepted §13.3 deviation carrying measurement and a
reopening path. N4 is immutable, never an item that opens or closes. Verification complete joins P4-CVG,
T3, and Tier D at the phase-4 exit. Rule rather than schedule closers are phase-3 inventory items
(`SP-PROBE-2` → X6 `HyperBinaryRes`, plan §5.2); §2 separately owns guard purity.

**Cross-solver divergence gate (2026-08-09; strengthened by P4-CVG).** Exact
instruction identity is not required: representations and deliberate mechanisms
differ. Each first portable divergence records bounded context, cause, strength,
disposition, evidence, reproducer, owner, and reopening condition. Comparator
status advances `localized` → `actionable` → `closed`; neither of the first two
closes a fixture. An identical prefix closes only if both traces terminate and
are untruncated. Comparison may resume past a closed root only at a named common
anchor; downstream differences otherwise inherit that root. Diagnostic builds
use `TACTIX_ENABLE_EVENT_TRACE=ON`; admitted preprocessing rules emit auxiliary
begin/end `slot_boundary` snapshots. Ordinary builds omit trace implementation,
symbols, and event strings. The pinned Kissat overlay lives under
`tools/solvers/variants/kissat-event-trace/`; comparator:
`tools/bench/compare_event_trace.py`.

### 13.1 The six themes — what the gaps have in common

Ranked by how much they explain. These, not the individual rows, are what a fix should target.

| | theme | shape |
|---|---|---|
| **T1** | **We port mechanisms; we drop the WIRES between them.** *The master pattern.* | Intra-rule fidelity is high; **every high-severity gap is a cross-mechanism wire.** Recognisable sub-shape in six rows: *we own the mechanism, the field is named after the reference's, and nothing writes it* (`factor_bound` — WIRE since closed by SP-FACTOR-1, kept as the exemplar that named the shape, `VIVIFY_TIER2` vs `k.tier2`, `factor_cand_rounds`, `factor_demote_fresh`, `bce_occ_limit`, `used_glue[1]`, `arena_mark`). **Worse than a missing mechanism, because grep finds it and reports parity.** |
