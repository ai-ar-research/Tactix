# Factor dosing study — frozen compact record

**Status:** historical reference and measurement record.

## Reference findings

Kissat couples Factor acceptance to the current BVE growth allowance, advances
that allowance on its elimination ladder, retains a candidate schedule across
the relevant phase, and meters work in a cache-line-shaped currency. These
mechanisms must be ported together: an inert or zero-valued bound can make a
nominal coupling arm byte-identical and therefore non-evidence.

## Tactix disposition

The BVE/Factor bound coupling, ladder traversal, private Factor meter, candidate
rounds, and explicit admission channel have landed. The former binary-density
gate was deleted because neither reference has it and its numerator counted
historical proof identities rather than the live population. Detailed defaults,
reach counters, accepted deviations, measurements, and reopening paths are in
`docs/implementation-notes.md` under `SP-FACTOR-1`.

This snapshot owns no current action. Dated reports preserve the individual
ablation results; future changes start from the unconditional shipped baseline
and require a reach witness before performance interpretation.
