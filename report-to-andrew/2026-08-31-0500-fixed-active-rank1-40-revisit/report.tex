\section{Executive outcome}

The latest convergence pass found and repaired a general mismatch in the
meaning of an active variable.  Pinned Kissat deactivates a variable as soon
as it becomes fixed at level zero.  Tactix must retain the same root assignment
and stable variable identity for proof and model reconstruction, but its shared
population helper counted every non-retired identity as active.  It therefore
mixed semantic liveness with physical retention.

The repaired predicate excludes semantic level-zero fixed variables while
continuing to count non-root search assignments.  It introduces no option,
formula profile, classification consumer, or benchmark/rank gate.  A uniform
Rank 1--40 campaign solves every instance in 10,290.269 seconds, improving the
immediately preceding 10,573.791-second candidate by 2.7\% in total and 2.6\%
in geometric mean.  All 22 SAT models are independently verified.  At this
report snapshot 16 of 18 UNSAT proofs are verified and the remaining two
continue without blocking provisional solver timing; every completed record
is valid and residue-free.

The increment is positive but not convergence-complete.  SAT time improves
10.2\%, while UNSAT time regresses 4.2\%, chiefly because Rank 33 grows from
395.771 to 753.767 seconds.  Rank 17 improves substantially but remains 2.58x
Kissat.  Peak memory is unchanged and still owned by Rank 10.

\section{How Rank 17 exposed the mismatch}

The preceding Rank-17 investigation localized a 104.637-to-385.402-second
trajectory change to the per-candidate Eliminate/BVE publication of root
assignments, pivot retirements, and propagation roots into the decision heap.
That publication matches Kissat and was retained because it had already made
Rank 34's complete BVE removal sequence exact.  The next question was the state
presented to that correct lifecycle.

A conventional global event trace was not usable: both solvers timed out even
at 900 seconds and emitted only a header.  The replacement is a Trace-only
standalone observer which performs no work until the first BVE schedule, writes
one symmetric summary and optional member rows, then exits before the first
candidate.  It has no Release behavior or production consumer.

The exact member comparison found 328,081 scheduled Tactix variables and
328,795 Kissat variables.  They share 322,663 members; 5,418 occur only in
Tactix and 6,132 only in Kissat.  Among common members, 72,684 have different
physical occurrence pairs, every score differs, and 320,706 heap positions
differ.  The schedule is therefore already different before the retained
per-candidate publication runs.

At first BVE entry the conflict boundary is nearly shared (18,268 versus
18,269), but the physical states are not.  Tactix has a 616,255-entry retained
root trail, 1,910,024 irredundant clauses, and 66,106 binaries; Kissat has a
5,593-entry trail, 1,699,758 irredundant clauses, and 254,564 binaries.  Tactix
reported 944,336 active variables although exactly 328,081 were unassigned and
scheduled.  The retained roots account for the discrepancy.

\section{Reference semantics and repair}

Pinned code{flags.c::kissat\_mark\_fixed\_literal} sets the fixed flag and
immediately calls code{deactivate\_variable}.  That transition clears active,
decrements code{solver->active}, dequeues the variable, and removes it from
the stable heap.  Ordinary search assignment does not perform this transition,
so a non-root assigned variable remains active.

Tactix's code{kernel\_active\_variables} now implements that semantic image
without physically discarding retained reconstruction state.  Focused coverage
pins both a retained root assignment as inactive and a non-root decision as
active.

The complete production-consumer audit found exactly the pre-existing Kissat
consumers:

\begin{itemize}
\item Factor's code{factordelay} population predicate;
\item Substitute's productive-round test; and
\item Probe/Eliminate outer fixed-point tests.
\end{itemize}

Two additional uses are Trace output only.  Heuristic queue membership,
variable collection population, and BVE candidate membership remain separate
physical representations.  No new consumer was added.

Causal job 1657636 proves the repair changes only the first-BVE summary from
code{active=944336} to code{active=328081}.  Every schedule hash, formula
count, score hash, decision count, and propagation count remains identical to
the Tactix-before authority at that boundary.  Focused Release job 1657638 then
solves Rank 17 with a verified model in 311.993 seconds versus 449.150 before.

\section{Memory and codebase revisit}

Peak sampled process-tree RSS is unchanged at 16,987,860 KiB and remains Rank
10.  This repair changes semantic population accounting, not physical storage;
it does not remove retained clauses, watches, variable-indexed arrays, or proof
and model identities.  Medium instances also remain commonly two or more times
Kissat's RSS.  Rank 10 representation ownership remains the memory priority.

The codebase revisit audited every active-population consumer against pinned
Kissat and separated four concepts which should not be collapsed: semantic
active population, physical heuristic membership, collection population, and
BVE occurrence membership.  The production change remains deliberately small.
The bounded schedule observer is retained only as diagnostic infrastructure;
its environment names are absent from Release.

Release passes 76/76 and Trace 75/75 after the revisit.  N1--N4, native proof
accountability, the proof-rule/strategy boundary, Kissat creation order, and
uniform BVE/Factor/forward-subsumption/Vivify defaults remain intact.  The
removed Dose5, Factor5, NoBve, NoBveLight, and StartupFactorOnly profiles remain
absent.

\section{Decision and next work}

Retain the active-state repair as a reference-correct and aggregate-positive
increment.  Do not add an exception for Rank 33 or roll back the repair.  The
next runtime owner is Rank 33's first downstream divergence; Rank 17 remains an
upstream physical formula/root-compaction/search-state investigation.  Rank 10
remains the first portable memory target.

Rank 1--40 evaluation, codebase/mechanism revisit, progress update, and major
Andrew report.  The remaining independent proof checks continue as a final
