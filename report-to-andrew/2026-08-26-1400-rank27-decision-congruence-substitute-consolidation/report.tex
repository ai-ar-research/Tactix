\section{Changes and boundaries}
\paragraph{Decision journal.}
Production builds no longer construct and shift the dynamic
\code{literal=} candidate string for every decision when the rule journal is
compiled out.  Journal-enabled builds construct the same string lazily and
retain exact record/replay identity.  The decision transaction, branching
policy, heap state, scheduling, and proof behavior are unchanged.

\paragraph{Congruence lifecycle.}
Forward subsumption reconnects the live large-watch image and defers physical
arena reclamation to the caller's established phase boundary.  This removes a
duplicate full-arena pack immediately before Substitute.  The live clause
order is unchanged, stable references remain usable, no watch points into
garbage, and the later boundary still owns forwarding and arena-generation
invalidation.  A strengthened standalone test checks both pre-boundary and
post-collection states.

\paragraph{Substitute affected owners.}
Canonical Substitute marks every retiring literal and every binary mirror
owner incident to it.  It retains the former global import/sign order and
per-list order over precisely that induced population, avoiding global scans
and compactions of unrelated watch lists.  Sweep/session behavior, SCC work,
proof work, candidate order, large-clause rewriting, and collection remain
unchanged.

These remain representation and lifecycle repairs below the proof-rule/
strategy boundary.  N1--N4, exact logical tick/cost, candidate order,
scheduling order, learning, pruning, and retained semantic state remain the
acceptance surface.
