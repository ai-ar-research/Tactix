\section*{Objective and outcome}
The requested audit covered Vivify admission, scheduling, candidate and
literal order, interaction with reason jumping, terminal proof rules, and
interleaving with the surrounding inprocessing pipeline.  The concern was
correct: Tactix carried an invented size-100 candidate cutoff absent from both
reference solvers.  Removing it exposed three additional coupled differences
from Kissat: independent rather than cumulative tier budgets, premature
expansion of a jumped binary reason during semantic deduction, and a
replacement-only productivity numerator that delayed away the next
irredundant round.

The production repair follows the reference mechanism without input identity
or tuning.  It removes the cutoff, charges schedule and watch rebuilding to a
single cumulative Vivify ceiling, reads the virtual jumped reason for semantic
subsumer detection while retaining real-antecedent proof expansion, and
counts every successful terminal in productivity.  The final common-state
trace restores Kissat's second irredundant round, exact long-clause counts, and
exact aggregate terminal results.  Every binary-producing probe terminal also
has the same effect.

Release passes 72/72 tests.  The mandatory 28B+5C matched guard is 66/66 valid;
all models and proofs check and no certificate remains.  This is a semantic
and correctness closure, not yet a performance-pin acceptance: the candidate
takes 1.241 times the accepted pin's aggregate time.  The development plan
therefore treats the timing regression as evidence of a still-missing coupling
and forbids restoring the hack.

\section*{The four source-backed defects}
At conflict 3069, the retained traces have a common semantic formula and equal
long/binary populations before Vivify.

\paragraph{Candidate scope.}
Tactix's first skipped changed candidate had 435 literals.  The only cause was
\code{VIVIFY\_CLSLIM=100}.  Kissat \code{vivify.c} has no candidate-size test;
its propagation-tick ceiling bounds work.  After deleting the cutoff, the
first 32 candidate identities, priorities, order keys, and probe poses match.
A 101-literal focused regression prevents the invented policy from returning.

\paragraph{Cumulative scheduling.}
Kissat computes one absolute probing-tick allowance, then advances its ceiling
by the 3/3/1 redundant shares and the irredundant share.  Unused early work is
available later, while an early overrun is precharged.  Tactix gave every band
a fresh independent budget and started its clock after schedule/watch work.
Two policy fields now carry the already-spent offset and distinguish bounded
zero from an ordinary unbounded call.  A synthetic tier-3 fixture requires
unused tier-1/tier-2 budget to carry.

\paragraph{Jumped semantic reason.}
The first terminal split was candidate hash \code{1c3734085b121754}, literals
\code{(-480909,480954,480955)}.  Kissat subsumed it; Tactix rewrote it to a
binary.  The assignment chain was
\code{480909:binary(96248) | 96248:binary(480954) | 480954:decision}.
Reason jumping stores the virtual resolvent
\code{(-480909 or 480954)}.  Tactix expanded through 96248 before semantic
subsumer detection, hiding that resolvent.  Deduction now reads the virtual
reason; native proof walks still expand both real antecedents.  A focused test
checks both the subsumption and the proof-safe separation.

\paragraph{Productivity and interleaving.}
After the semantic repair, Tactix still ran seven Vivify rounds while Kissat
ran eight.  Its irredundant yield counted replacement clauses only, omitting
7,982 subsumptions.  That raised the delay and skipped the next irredundant
band.  The numerator now includes subsumption, shrink, implied/asymmetric
deletion, and instantiation, matching Kissat's per-candidate
\code{vivifier.vivified}.  A subsumption-only scheduling regression requires
the delay to fall rather than rise.

\section*{Limitations and next action}
The repaired code closes the identified Vivify semantics at common-input
candidate, schedule, deduction, and emitted-binary layers.  It does not claim
that every later rank-27 search transaction matches Kissat, nor that the
candidate is a new accepted performance pin.  If rank 27 remains open, the
next UNSAT-first action is to continue from the first portable post-Vivify
divergence, without repeating candidate-scope, cumulative-budget,
jumped-reason, or productivity investigations.  Separately, the predeclared
\code{mp1-Nb5T15}/\code{sted5} regression must be localized as a missing
coupling before performance acceptance.  Any further source change again owes
the complete 28B+5C guard before another Tier-D witness.
