\section*{Executive summary}
This checkpoint corrects the performance authority for the promoted Vivify
arena-order representation repair.  The repair itself remains semantically
exact and positive.  However, the predecessor used by several initial
whole-run comparisons was later found to contain compiled tracing controls and
was not build-equivalent to the binary that produced the accepted 399.152-second
Rank-27 result.  Those comparisons remain useful for semantic evidence but are
excluded from performance authority.

A fresh archive build of commit \code{a4b11964} reproduces the historical
predecessor binary, SHA-256
\hashcode{60fe8eae742d185e96aac6cd6a94c49bccc3bf23a852464f729e63e2a16ef366},
bit for bit.  Clean candidate SHA-256 is
\hashcode{067f7f3a0566cce6a52c90d78228b51cc7444adadb464402d5339f07ed5070bd}.
Direct clean controls preserve the complete normalized non-time image and show
the candidate non-regressive on node017 and faster on node018.  Most
importantly, the exact unchanged predecessor ran in 553.948 seconds on node017
and 394.107 seconds on node018.  Thus ``recovering'' the approximately
399-second behavior required no solver change: it was recovered by running the
same executable on node018.  The 159.841-second same-binary spread assigns the
large absolute shift to execution environment or load, not source history.

The corrected clean frozen B28+C5 gate passes all 66 arms with every model or
proof independently checked, all 33 paired logical images exact, no solve
loss, and zero certificate residue.  Clean candidate/predecessor PAR-2 is
1542.691/1576.572 seconds (0.978510).  The Vivify repair is therefore promoted
on corrected authority.  Absolute Rank-27 runtime remains environmentally
sensitive and the 300-second milestone remains open.

\code{8001b7f...} and source-diff SHA-256

\section{Disposition and next actions}
The Vivify arena-order repair remains promoted.  It changes only the
representation used to restore the already-required stable logical arena
order; candidate order, exact ticks/costs, scheduling, proof effects, learning,
pruning, retained state, N1--N4, and the proof-rule/strategy boundary remain
unchanged.  All prior Sweep, directed-bridge, BVE antecedent-admission, and
Congruence closures remain closed.

The approximately 399-second behavior was recovered by placement, not by a
new solver edit, so it is not a stable performance guarantee.  Rank 27 remains
above the 300-second milestone and convergence continues.  The next owner is
the preserved residual-BVE flat-slice representation prototype, which already
has positive exact-state stage and clean frozen-gate evidence but remains
outside the active production source.  After independently completing its
Rank-27 and Claude-owned fuzz requirements, resume broad search-quality and
throughput localization against current instrumented Kissat.
