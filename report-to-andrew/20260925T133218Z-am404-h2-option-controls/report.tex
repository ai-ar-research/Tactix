AM404 expands the fixed controls for H2 without changing the solver or its canonical proof route. Three additional archived DSR proofs pass in both modes.

\section*{Soundness controls and provenance}
The option deliberately honors deletion of an implied-unit reason. A proof whose later witnessed unit depends on that deletion is valid with the option and invalid without it. Removing only that deletion makes the same witness invalid again: Trestle reports \code{s INVALID} under the option. A separate five-variable bad-witness proof is likewise rejected by Trestle. These controls establish that the changed acceptance in this fixed set is caused by the specified proof-state semantics, not a blanket checker success. They are not a proof that every certificate accepted under the option is sound.

Job \code{1721075} ran for three seconds on hwulab with two CPUs and 8 GiB. It used the AM369 pinned checker hashes, the repository's clause-preserving checker-CNF spelling, and both backward and forward proof elaboration; successful cases required Trestle. Exact inputs, wrapper, hashes, LSRs, logs and the 14-row result table are retained under \code{benchmarks/probes/am404-h2-option-corpus/}. The AM404 technical report records the case matrix and its limits.
