\section*{Executive summary}
This increment promotes a general representation repair in Congruence's
immutable small-clause index.  Canonical records are already lexicographically
sorted, so a queried key's first literal identifies one contiguous interval.
The new directory restricts the existing binary search to that interval.  It
does not alter record or gate order, candidate admission, logical ticks or
costs, scheduling, proof transactions, learning, pruning, or N1--N4.

The exact conflict-9450 Rank-27 profile reduces complete sampled cycles from
64.668B to 63.456B and the intended extraction owner from 2.914B to 1.355B.
All 64 event-trace rows are byte-identical.  The complete frozen B28 plus fixed
C5 guard passes 66/66 independently checked tasks, and corrected exact-source
ASan/UBSan native-LRAT fuzz passes 10,000 cases with zero failures.  Checked
Rank-27 job \code{1645875} verifies the byte-identical 1,514,395,111-byte DRAT
proof and removes it, improving internal time 407.006 to 403.855 seconds.
The repair is accepted, but Rank 27 remains open because the frozen target is
300 seconds.

Frozen Kissat 4.0.4 and all other frozen reference solvers were not rerun.
The user-canceled full Tier-D arrays remain frozen, non-scorable, and
residue-free; this increment uses only the predeclared Rank-27 input and
mandatory promotion guards.
