\section*{Executive summary}
This increment promotes a second general representation repair for Congruence
ITE extraction.  After the accepted ternary sign-mask repair, residual tracing
at the exact Rank-27 conflict-9450 common state found that Tactix spent 210 of
415 ITE milliseconds inserting 4,585,536 conditional pairs through an
\code{unordered_map}.  That collection alone cost 1.79 times current
instrumented Kissat's complete 117 ms ITE stage.  Kissat's source-shaped
counterpart uses literal-indexed stacks.

The repair maps each dense literal id directly to a sparse owned pair-vector
slot.  Only touched literals own vectors, and a sparse key list is sorted before
consumption exactly as before.  Canonical record traversal, per-literal append
order, candidate pairing and verification, logical ticks and budgets,
scheduling, gates, merges, units, proof transactions, learning, pruning, and
N1--N4 are unchanged.  No benchmark identity, rank, deadline, arm, or tuned
constant enters the solver.

At the exact common state, ITE falls from 572 to 184 ms, complete Congruence
from 2383 to 1953 ms, the solve prefix from 16517 to 16147 ms, and peak RSS from
406028 to 394776 KiB.  Total sampled cycles fall 3.32\%, and the exact 64-row
event/order trace remains byte-identical.  The complete frozen B28 plus fixed
C5 guard passes 66/66 independently checked tasks with zero residue.

Checked Rank-27 job \code{1646031} proves UNSAT in 399.152 internal / 399.668
wall seconds, versus 409.438 / 409.957 for the accepted predecessor.  The
1,514,395,111-byte proof is byte-identical and independently verified before
deletion; every non-time statistic is exact.  Congruence improves 16.154 to
14.720 seconds and peak solver RSS falls 643976 to 604272 KiB.  Rank 27 remains
above the 300-second engineering milestone and frozen Kissat's 278.187 seconds,
so convergence continues.

The full Tier-D arrays remain canceled and non-scorable.  A new repository
policy now assigns every future fuzz submission, monitoring pass, artifact
inspection, cleanup audit, and terminal report exclusively to Claude Code.
Codex will accept Claude's pass/failure report without probing fuzz artifacts;
failure returns directly to Claude's bounded repair workflow.
