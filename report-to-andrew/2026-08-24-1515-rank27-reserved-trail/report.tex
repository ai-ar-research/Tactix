\section*{Executive summary}
Current-Tactix/current-Kissat full-run profiling localized a portable
representation cost in Tactix's propagation path: every assignment called the
generic grow-capable \path{std::vector<unsigned>::push_back}, although variable
growth had already reserved enough trail space and search can assign at most one
literal per variable.  Kissat instead writes through a reserved raw-stack
cursor.  The general repair introduces a Kernel-owned pre-sized backing store
with a separate logical end.  It changes no proof rule, strategy admission,
schedule, logical effort currency, candidate order, or benchmark-specific arm.

At the exact conflict-9450 common state, the repair preserves every non-time
counter and the complete event trace while reducing sampled cycles by 4.31\%
and total time by 748 ms.  The mandatory frozen Tier B plus fixed Tier C guard
passes 66/66, and exact-source ASan/UBSan native-LRAT fuzz passes 10,000 cases
with zero failures.  The checked immutable Rank-27 witness preserves every
non-time statistic and the exact 1,514,395,111-byte proof while improving
413.830 to 407.006 internal seconds.  Pinned \code{drat-trim} verifies that
proof, and all campaigns leave zero certificate residue.  This is a validated
6.824-second same-trajectory recovery, but Rank 27 remains open at 1.357 times
the 300-second engineering target.

\subsection*{General representation repair}
\code{ReservedTrail} owns a pre-sized \code{std::vector<Lit>} plus a logical
\code{used} end.  \code{reserve} enlarges backing size only on variable growth;
\code{push_back} asserts spare storage and performs one indexed store;
backtrack changes only the logical end.  Variable compaction adopts the
replacement vector allocation and exposes its spare capacity as backing size
before search resumes.  Iteration, indexing, conversion, and Kernel clients see
only the live prefix.  A focused regression covers reserve, push, iteration,
conversion, shrink, pop, compaction-vector adoption, and post-adoption push.

This contract follows an existing semantic invariant---at most one assignment
per variable, with reservation before any new variable can be assigned.  It
does not introduce a capacity constant or a formula classifier.  The exact
Release candidate SHA-256 is
\hashcode{b6c1623b89477b140cabddb3cb139ca797778a871aeb67ab35fc708c3cd87851};
its source diff SHA-256 is

\section{Risks and decisions}
The improvement is real but smaller over the full solve than the early 4.31\%
cycle reduction: 407.006 seconds remains 1.357 times the engineering threshold
and 128.819 seconds behind frozen Kissat.  The paired full profiles use
different complete trajectories, so broad percentages remain localization
leads only.  Any next repair still requires an exact common-state price plus
exact candidate/order, scheduling, logical cost, and pruning comparison.

The repair is promoted because it is general, source-backed, trajectory-exact,
proof-exact, and independently guarded.  Completed semantic investigations are
not reopened: no evidence contradicts Sweep parity, the directed bridge fix,
BVE admission, or Vivify's normalized duplicate cleanup.  Frozen Kissat is not
rerun, and current instrumented Kissat remains diagnostic-only.

At user direction the asynchronous full Tier-D and surviving predecessor were
canceled earlier to free capacity.  Their frozen partial classifications remain
non-scorable; resource and checker exits are not mislabeled as solver
correctness failures, and both campaigns have zero pending certificate residue.
