\section*{Executive summary}
This increment promotes a general representation repair for Congruence ITE
extraction.  At the exact conflict-9450 Rank-27 common state, current Tactix
spent 630 ms in ITE extraction versus current instrumented Kissat's 117 ms.
Tactix asked six exact ternary membership questions for each canonical
small-clause record; Kissat obtained the same sign combinations from
literal-local dense watches.  The repair groups immutable records by their
unsigned three-variable prefix and caches one byte containing the eight exact
ternary sign patterns.  Canonical iteration, candidate order, exact ticks and
costs, scheduling, proof transactions, learning, pruning, and N1--N4 are
unchanged.

The exact profile reduces the separately materialized membership lambda from
475.813M to 130.659M cycles, complete gate extraction from 983.981M to
714.124M, and whole-prefix cycles from 63.890B to 63.485B.  All 64 event-trace
rows are byte-identical.  The complete frozen B28 plus fixed C5 guard passes
66/66 independently checked tasks, and exact-source ASan/UBSan native-LRAT
fuzz passes 10,000 random cases with zero failures.

Checked Rank-27 job \code{1645956} verifies the byte-identical
1,514,395,111-byte DRAT proof and removes it.  Every non-time statistic is
exact; Congruence improves 16.910 to 16.154 seconds.  Total internal time is
409.438 seconds versus 403.855 for the predecessor because unchanged-trajectory
search/scaffolding is 5.259 seconds noisier.  The intended owner improvement,
exact common-state cycle reduction, and full correctness gates justify
promotion.  The 300-second Rank-27 threshold is an engineering milestone, not
a stopping condition: convergence continues while portable current-Kissat
gaps remain.

Frozen Kissat 4.0.4 and all other frozen reference solvers were not rerun.  The
user-canceled full Tier-D arrays remain frozen, non-scorable, and residue-free.

\section{Repair and mandatory failure workflow}
After \code{ClauseIndex} construction, sorting, and deduplication, the accepted
repair sorts a temporary vector of record ordinals by the unsigned first-three-
variable prefix.  Each group accumulates one bit for each exact size-three sign
pattern and publishes that byte beside each record in original canonical
order.  ITE collection reads the byte only for distinct-variable records in
that same prefix.  Repeated-variable degeneracies and later cross-prefix
four-clause candidate verification retain the authoritative first-literal
range lookup.  The temporary four-byte ordinal per record is released after
construction; the scoped immutable index retains one byte per record.
