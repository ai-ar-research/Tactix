\section*{Objective and outcome}
The user asked that Tactix's Vivify behavior be compared with Kissat across
admission, scheduling, candidate order, and interleaving with other proof
rules, and requested this Andrew-facing report after the issue was fixed.  The
first policy defect was indeed Vivify-related: formula classification disabled
Vivify for selected size ranges even though Kissat admits it unconditionally
and governs work internally.  That gate was removed in the preceding
increment.  The rank-27 investigation then continued from the latest common
semantic state instead of assuming every later split was also owned by
Vivify.

After the reference-wide Congruence repair, Tactix and Kissat match through the
opening formula, 741,305 decisions, and conflict-2027 analysis.  The first new
portable divergence is a focused Restart: Kissat restarts at conflict 2027,
while Tactix waits until conflict 2028.  Their glue averages, admission values,
deadlines, learned clause, and backtrack target are exact.  The sole earlier
difference is that Tactix counted Rephase's root-trail clear as a canonical
Restart; Kissat does not.  That one extra event crosses the shipped
logarithmic interval boundary.

The smallest repair separates the shared sound backtrack mechanic from
canonical Restart accounting.  Rephase now uses the accounting-neutral
mechanic, while scheduled Restarts retain all prior accounting and trail-reuse
behavior.  The repair passes its boundary unit test, restores exact trace
behavior through conflict 2028, and passes the mandatory 28B+5C matched guard
66/66 with zero certificate residue.  The repaired guard is materially faster
in aggregate (0.7864 of the accepted pin), but the immutable rank-27 witness
still times out UNKNOWN at 1,200.004 seconds.  This is therefore a validated
general repair, not a rank-27 closure claim.

\section*{Source-backed cause}
Pinned Kissat \code{rephase.c} calls
\code{kissat\_backtrack\_propagate\_and\_flush\_trail}, increments
\code{rephased}, and never increments \code{statistics.restarts}.  Pinned
\code{restart.c} increments \code{statistics.restarts} only inside
\code{kissat\_restart}, then performs trail reuse/backtracking and updates the
focused limit.  Its focused interval is
\[
  \texttt{restartint} + \log_{10}(\texttt{restarts}+9)-1,
\]
with shipped \code{restartint=1} and integer conversion at the deadline.
At 90 Restarts, \(\log_{10}(99)-1<1\), so the added term truncates to zero and
the interval remains one conflict.  At 91, \(\log_{10}(100)-1=1\), so the
interval grows to two.  Old Tactix routed Rephase's neutral trail clear through
\code{restart\_apply\_to(k,0)}, crossing that exact boundary one event early.

This is a general lifecycle-accounting defect, not an empirical timing
threshold.  It explains both the one-conflict deadline shift and why all glue
EMA state could remain exact.

\section*{Implementation decision}
\artifact{src/rules/restart/restart.cpp} now exposes two layers:
\begin{itemize}
\item \code{restart\_backtrack\_to} clamps the requested level and performs the
  sound trail abandonment without Restart/reuse accounting;
\item \code{restart\_apply\_to} retains canonical accounting and delegates the
  shared mechanic.
\end{itemize}
\artifact{src/policies/default\_policy.cpp} uses the neutral layer only for
Rephase's root clear.  Scheduled Restarts still consume the branching-policy
pick, choose their reuse level, update target phases, increment statistics,
update the focused deadline, and emit the same lifecycle events.  Rephase
remains an H-only policy operation.

\artifact{tests/test\_rephase.cpp} pins the first interval transition.  Starting
at 90 canonical Restarts and conflict 2000, a Rephase clear must preserve count
90 and next conflict 2001.  One real Restart must advance to 91 and next
conflict 2002.  This directly catches the old conflation while remaining
formula- and instance-independent.

\section*{Vivify conclusion, limitations, and next action}
The Vivify audit produced a real policy correction: formula classification no
longer disables a reference-default proof rule.  The subsequent common-state
comparison also shows why convergence work cannot stop at the mechanism first
suspected.  On rank 27, the earliest remaining portable split after corrected
Congruence occurs later in search and belongs to Rephase/Restart accounting;
a Vivify-specific target arm would not have repaired its producer.

This increment establishes exactness only through conflict 2028, not for the
rest of rank 27's trajectory.  The next concrete action is to extend the paired
semantic and lifecycle trace only beyond that transaction, locate the first
later common-state divergence, and implement a further change only when the
source supports a general repair.  Any new candidate again owes the full Tier
B plus predeclared Tier C guard before another rank-27 witness.
