\section*{Outcome}
The general little-endian Sweep phase expansion now uses the eight masked
64-bit word values used by upstream Kitten, copied through alignment-safe
\code{memcpy}.  The scalar implementation remains the fallback on other byte
orders.  Exact tracing proves that random draws, all 64 phase bytes, rule and
candidate order, ticks, scheduling, learning, pruning, and the resulting
logical state are unchanged.  Complete correctness, performance, proof, model,
N1--N4, and Claude-owned fuzz gates pass.  Rank 27 is therefore parked; the
next convergence witness will come from a fresh current-Tactix/current-Kissat
comparison over immutable ranks 1--40.
