\section*{Executive outcome}
The latest cumulative Tactix pin keeps the accepted Rank-27 search trajectory
and cuts checked wall time from 579.772 to 504.297 seconds.  BVE falls from
174.204 to 94.389 seconds.  The repaired 1.633-GB DRAT certificate verifies and
is deleted, but the result remains 1.681 times the 300-second objective and
1.76 times the 286.30-second current-Kissat diagnostic control.

The improvement comes from general representation fidelity to Kitten.  BVE's
definition variables are already dense in first-import order, so the shared
subsolver no longer rebuilds an identical outer map.  It stores only the
watched occurrence structure that Kitten's model-flip path reads.  Finally,
second-core membership uses persistent scratch and retained original clauses
compact in place, matching \code{kitten_shrink_to_clausal_core} without
changing Tactix's proof-visible clause header.  No benchmark identity, rank,
hash, deadline, per-instance arm, or tuned constant enters the solver.

The apparent next semantic lead in conflict-9,449 Vivify is now closed as a
false positive.  Candidate order, decisions, and normalized logical ticks are
exact over 4,915 paired candidates.  Full binary-multiset snapshots show that
both solvers derive the same 19 binaries.  Tactix's scalar count ends one lower
only because it enters with two copies of one pair and normalizes to one,
whereas Kissat enters and exits with one.  No Vivify repair is justified.

\section*{Why the Vivify count was not a pruning loss}
Macro and rule-boundary traces agree through the second conflict-9,449 Vivify
entry.  Tactix exits with 753,322 binary copies and Kissat with 753,323, while
both have 574,136 irredundant and 1,902 redundant large clauses.  A scalar
reading therefore suggested that Tactix learned one fewer binary.

Campaign
\artifact{benchmarks/runs/20260824-0323-tierd-rank27-current-vivify9449-binary-sets/}
enumerates sorted permanent-external-literal multisets at every Vivify
boundary.  During the second invocation each solver adds the exact same 19
copies.  Kissat deletes 11 and Tactix deletes 12.  The sole extra Tactix
deletion is pair $(-354369,-354368)$: Tactix has two copies at entry and one at
exit; Kissat has one at both boundaries.  The two terminal multisets retain a
separate, inherited equivalent-representative difference, but the Vivify delta
contains no lost consequence.  This demonstrates why worse-first divergence
analysis must compare exact deltas, not only populations.

The diagnostic snapshot is disabled by default and exists only in event-trace
builds.  It retains duplicate copies, sorts by permanent external literals,
and changes neither proof rules nor scheduling.  Its Tactix path has a unit
test; the Kissat overlay has a static construction test.  Both diagnostic jobs
complete in under 47 seconds and leave no staged input or certificate.
