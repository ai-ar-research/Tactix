\section*{Outcome}
The recent native-LRAT hint audit found three producer-level defects. Instantiate
could cite a root unit already assumed by its lemma cube; Strengthen and BVE
could cite the same root unit twice. Those were repaired with focused causal
regressions. A fourth issue in Sweep's general binary ladder reused a
subsolver chain after lifting its lemma by a target literal. The chain is now
replayed under the \emph{complete} negated published lemma while the original
environment still exists. Already-satisfied hints are removed, and a chain
that cannot close is declined before any kernel mutation. This is a general
proof condition, not an instance-specific workaround. One proposed BVE
definition-unit change was disproved by the exact counterexample and reverted.

\section*{Evidence and limits}
On four unchanged original-CNF handoffs, a pinned Debug public-API run (SLURM
job 1722204, four CPUs, 32 GiB) emits proofs with zero strict-LRAT audit
failures; CakeLPR independently verifies all four. The BVE case includes
41 audited RAT lines. Fresh Release focused tests pass 7/7, and ASan+UBSan
focused rule tests pass 4/4. Exact commands, hashes, and checker logs are in the
\href{../../benchmarks/runs/20260925-am421-final-hint-check/}{AM421 run}; the
analysis is in the \href{../../benchmarks/reports/2026-09-25-am421-lifted-lrat-and-drat-audit.md}{AM421 technical report}.
These tests do not replace full current-source fuzz or the combined N1/N2 gate.

I independently classified the three AM400 DRAT strict-audit flags. In each,
every proof addition before the first root contradiction is RUP or RAT; the
audit flags only a later trailing empty addition after deletions have removed
that contradiction from its reconstructed population. The valid refuting
prefix remains sufficient, and drat-trim's backward and forward checks plus
CakeLPR verify the proofs. This is an audit-harness state error after terminal
refutation, not a new solver inference defect. The historical campaign is
unchanged; any shared audit repair needs positive and negative self-tests.
The AM400 Probe/Lucky owner has terminal affected-scope PASS, not universal
N1/N2 acceptance. AM401, AM412, and AM414 owners remain active on their own
immutable pins.
