\section*{Outcome}
AM407 compared the historical no-option DSR checker route with
\texttt{--delete-implied-units} on four archived, real Tactix CNF/proof pairs.
All seven predeclared outcomes matched; the observation-only eighth also
verified. One proof verifies only with the
option; another verifies only without it; a known Sweep-retention regression
is rejected by both; and its repaired exact case verifies under both. This
is stronger contract evidence than hand-built controls alone, but neither
mode has been promoted or proved universally sound.

\section*{Verification}
Job \texttt{1721174} completed \texttt{0:0} in three seconds on hwulab with
two CPUs and 8 GiB. Both modes used the same frozen AM369
\texttt{dsr-trim}, \texttt{dsr-compress} and Trestle pins. A pass required
backward elaboration, independent Trestle verification, and then forward
every-lemma elaboration and Trestle verification. The AM296 direct-SR proof
under no-option shows why the independent stage matters: backward elaboration
produces an LSR, but Trestle reports \texttt{s INVALID}. The opposite AM296
proof under the option fails at its proof line 13 because the requested
deletion removes an implied-unit reason it later needs.

The exact runner, eight original inputs, per-stage logs, resulting LSRs and
SHA-256 manifests are frozen in the \texttt{am407-dsr-archived-contract}
probe under \texttt{benchmarks/probes/}. The AM407 technical report in
\texttt{benchmarks/reports/} records the eight-row comparison and limits.
No solver, production oracle, checker binary, or public API changed.

\section*{Decision and remaining work}
These results make the choice consequential: the option is not a monotone
relaxation. The canonical no-option route remains unchanged while the user
decides whether to adopt a fully audited option contract or require a
general checker-visible proof-state repair. H2 still fails no-option;
post-finalization public clause addition can still produce a wrong SAT model.
The Opus-owned AM400/401 fuzz legs and two full Debug suites are in flight.
N1--N4, the retained Rank/B+C/WV gates, and production online-learning
readiness are not complete. The requested full Tier-D comparison at 5,000
seconds, four CPUs and 32 GiB on hwulab is therefore not submitted.
