\section*{Outcome}
On the same archived \texttt{or\_randxor} UNSAT input, LymphoSAT reproduced its
157,075-byte verified PBP proof while Tactix produced a 7,770,045-byte native
DSR proof. Both certificates independently verify. This is a concrete
proof-representation/technique gap, not missing semantic UNSAT coverage and
not an N2 failure. AM410 separately showed Tactix's startup PBP already
covers the exact Tseitin winner witness; the general post-startup PB action
interface remains absent.

\section*{Evidence and failed first check}
Both solvers ran sequentially in hwulab job \texttt{1721443} with four CPUs
and 32 GiB. LymphoSAT selected \texttt{or\_randxor}, emitted the exact
baseline task-414 PBP bytes, and passed pinned VeriPB and CakePB. Tactix
source \texttt{6c176683} exited UNSAT after 121,749 conflicts in 4.45 seconds,
with zero startup parity recognition. Its first proof-check attempt was never
run: the diagnostic script omitted the checker work directory, so the job
ended \texttt{FAILED 1:0} on a file-creation error. The omission is fixed in
the probe runner, not in production code.

Checker-only job \texttt{1721451} used the unchanged exact CNF and DSR proof,
completed \texttt{0:0}, and passed frozen no-option DSR-to-LSR elaboration
with independent Trestle checking in both backward and forward every-lemma
audits. The AM411 technical report and compressed evidence under
\texttt{benchmarks/} retain all commands, full original proofs, checks and
hashes. A redundant 33 MB archive of reproducible derived LSRs was removed;
the raw local files and lean 3.1 MB evidence archive remain.

\section*{Interpretation and next actions}
The same-allocation times were 0.02 seconds for LymphoSAT and 4.45 seconds
for Tactix, but one sequential row is not a competitiveness result. The PB
certificate is about 49.5 times smaller. A general typed PB derivation and
format/state contract could address such gaps; a family-name dispatcher or
ad hoc PBP/DSR splice would violate the shared-rule architecture. Finish the
active Opus fuzz and full Debug verdicts, repair the remaining H2 and
post-finalization API bugs, then rerun combined N1--N4 and Rank/B+C/WV gates.
The requested full Tier-D 5,000-second run remains deferred until those
conditions are met. Production online learning still needs the shared
default-exact slot controller and safe-point owned sub-strategy switching.
