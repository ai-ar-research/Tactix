\paragraph{Outcome.} The minimized H2 certificate still fails Tactix's
canonical, no-option DSR checker. A frozen checker trace now identifies the
precise conflict: the checker retains original clause $\lnot18\lor19$ after
Tactix retires it, derives root unit $19$, and then encounters an emitted SR
witness that maps $19$ to false. The technical evidence and exact commands are
in the AM439 report under \texttt{benchmarks/reports/}. No solver or checker
contract was changed.

\paragraph{Why this matters.} Publishing $19$ explicitly or dropping the
first conflicting witness mapping does not repair the sequence: later SR
obligations still conflict with the checker-visible root. We tested successive
checker-only witness omissions and observed the failure move to later
additions. This does not prove all possible derivations impossible. It does
show that a general N2 repair must account for the checker's retained
population or use an independently checkable alternative derivation while
preserving each legal public X7 action. The separate
\texttt{--delete-implied-units} mode verifies H2, but earlier archived proofs
show that it is not a monotone relaxation. The canonical mode remains
unchanged pending a deliberate contract decision and full audit.

\paragraph{Other gates.} Claude/Opus has now filed terminal affected-scope

\paragraph{Next action.} Keep the no-option contract fixed while resolving

not classify affected-scope fuzz or partial checker jobs as full acceptance.
