\section{Disposition and next work}
The candidate satisfies its exact-stage, exact-trace, full Rank-27, complete
B28+C5, clean-source rebuild, focused local validation, and checked proof
contracts.  Promotion now requires only Claude Code's authoritative fuzz PASS
and the final Release/report wrap-up; both are now complete.  The public total
selector remains the N1
authority; the optimized entry points belong only to DefaultPolicy sites whose
active guard is explicit.

After this increment, the next source-backed broad owner is collection and
rewatching.  A conservative current-Kissat conflict-10000 profile spends
4.521B cycles in explicit collection/flush/rewatch operations, while the
shorter exact Tactix prefix spends 7.451B.  Existing Tactix disassembly assigns
about 60\% of \code{kernel_collect_garbage}'s local samples to a stable watch
compaction followed by a second retained-entry walk that only forwards large
Refs.  A fused compact-and-forward primitive is the next bounded representation
hypothesis, but no prototype starts before this increment is independently
landed.  After collection/rewatching, matched Substitute invocations are the
next direct localization target, followed by the smaller residual Congruence
difference and continuing broad search-quality convergence.  Each repair is a
separate exact-prefix, Rank-27/B+C, full checked gate, Claude fuzz, and report
increment.
