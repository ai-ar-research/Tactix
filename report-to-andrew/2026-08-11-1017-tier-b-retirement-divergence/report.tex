\section*{Executive summary}

The user-directed Tier B divergence block is complete. A matched full-tier
comparison improves \tactix{} from 21/28 to 25/28 solved and from 187.33 to
116.58 seconds PAR-2. The repair recovers four formerly unsolved fixtures:
\code{summle}, \code{circuit}, \code{201311}, and \code{b22}. Every scored SAT
model and UNSAT DRAT certificate passes its independent checker; the remaining
24 fixtures move by at most 0.42 seconds.

The defect was not an unfavorable Sweep schedule. Substitute and BVE can
report the same permanent variable retirement before DefaultPolicy drains the
event queue. \tactix{} assumed every report was the first and dequeued an
already disconnected VMTF node. That followed sentinel links, erased queue
endpoints, and eventually made focused branching read an invalid variable.
Heuristic deactivation is now idempotent.

\section{Causal evidence and decision}

The investigation selected an early SAT failure, a long-running SAT timeout,
an early UNSAT failure, and a held-out equivalence-heavy UNSAT fixture. GDB
localized the circuit crash to
\code{heuristics_queue_next_unassigned -> DefaultBranching::pick}; ASan
confirmed the invalid read in the focused queue walk. The crash reproduced
without proof logging and on the preceding P4 pin.

A one-variable-family ablation isolated retirement:

The accepted repair checks VMTF membership before removal. Repeated reports
still remove a stale stable-heap member and reset the focused cursor if needed,
but never mutate disconnected queue links. A dedicated counter fires seven
times on the production circuit fixture; that run returns SAT and its model
satisfies all 192,064 clauses. The regression test explicitly retires two
variables twice in both focused and stable modes.

A separate full-tier cut rejected disabling Sweep resumption: solved count
fell from 21 to 20 and PAR-2 rose from 186.22 to 197.08 seconds. Sweep remains
enabled and resumable.

\section{Limitations and next actions}

This is a large solved-count and correctness-adjacent repair, not Tier B
parity. The remaining primary divergence set is SAT \code{mod4block}, UNSAT
\code{FmlaEquivChain}, and SAT \code{PRP_30_38}. A secondary trace found that
\kissat{} derives an initial \code{b22} Congruence equivalence and immediately
substitutes variable 14812 by \code{-10713}, while \tactix{} enters Sweep on
the unreduced formula. That missed gate/equivalence is source-backed but was
not required to accept this repair.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Use the three remaining Tier B timeouts as the next fixed divergence
        set; compare work and unit cost before changing a second mechanism.
  \item Retain the \code{b22} Congruence miss as the secondary frontier for its
        residual 118.95s versus 9.59s gap.
  \item Resume N4 verification after the user-directed Tier B work, then the
        N1 evidence repairs and Tier D/T3 sequence recorded in \code{PROGRESS.md}.
\end{enumerate}

Tier C was deliberately cancelled and no conclusion is drawn from its partial
artifacts. The sparse-GC candidate and tracked corpus cases 004/005 were not
run or inspected in this block.

The detailed causal and benchmark record is
\code{benchmarks/reports/2026-08-11-tier-b-retirement-divergence.md}.
