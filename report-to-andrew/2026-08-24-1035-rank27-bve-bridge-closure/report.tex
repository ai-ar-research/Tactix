\section*{DRAT bridge-population defect and repair}
The first frozen current-head Tier-D run was canceled after task 21,
\code{goldcrest-and-11}, emitted a 968,410,268-byte DRAT proof rejected at line
13,564,647.  Exact campaign
\artifact{benchmarks/runs/20260824-0337-tierd-goldcrest-proof-reproducer/}
reproduces that DRAT stream byte-for-byte while native LRAT independently
verifies a 10.207-GB proof.  Thus the UNSAT derivation is sound and the defect
is DRAT's deletion-by-literal population, not a solver contradiction or
checker limitation.  All large certificates and decompressed inputs were
deleted after checking.

Under the repository's required soundness workflow, authorized Claude Code
instrumented the binary lifecycle and localized the defect before its account
quota stopped the session.  Sweep can create a preserved equivalence bridge
whose transformed target pair coincides with an ordinary binary.  The proof
pass consumes that ordinary copy, but old physical compaction exempted every
entry sharing the bridge pair.  The kernel therefore retained a clause after
the checker population had exhausted all of its copies, and a later lemma
using the survivor failed RUP.

The repair counts exact directed entries.  An unchanged bridge protects its
two physical directions, a bridge with one moved endpoint protects only the
stable direction created by the in-place rewrite, and a bridge with both
endpoints moved protects none before rebuilding.  Ordinary copies that merely
share a literal pair follow the normal rewrite/removal path.  Regression
\code{test_sweep_bridge_target_pair_collision_consumes_ordinary_copy} checks
both kernel multiplicity and DRAT population balance.  Release and ASan/UBSan
Substitute and Sweep tests pass independently.

Exact repaired-source campaign
\artifact{benchmarks/runs/20260824-0339-goldcrest-bridge-count-repair/}
returns UNSAT and ordinary \code{drat-trim} verifies the
1,031,181,788-byte proof, SHA-256
\hashcode{c4f93869031ea1327c585906ebdb6f14c8730b6d4be4cb53af1ca4f478cab513},
in 261.516 seconds.  It uses 242,652,058 resolution steps, reports zero RAT
lemmas in the core, and leaves no proof, decompressed input, checker product,
or scratch residue.  Claude's small lifecycle trace is preserved and hashed;
its partial proof, input, and throwaway worktree are removed.
