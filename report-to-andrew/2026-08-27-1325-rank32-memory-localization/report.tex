completed diagnostic returned UNSAT, passed independent DRAT verification, and
left zero certificate and scratch residue.  No solver repair is promoted.

\section{Architectural interpretation}

Current Kissat reuses its per-literal watch vectors for dense occurrences.
Tactix's flat sparse-watch arena is a different hot representation and cannot
serve as the dense per-literal lists, so Tactix owns a separate vector-of-vectors.
That is a concrete architectural reason for roughly 93 MiB plus allocator
metadata, not evidence of a leaked rule-local scratch object.

A flat dense layout could reduce vector headers, allocation count, rounding,
and metadata, but its observed ceiling is only tens of MiB and the rewrite
would span broad inprocessing mutation paths.  It is therefore deferred until
cross-instance evidence makes that risk worthwhile.

The lower-risk reconstruction reserve opportunity is also bounded.  An exact
non-power-of-two Substitute reserve seed later doubles to 22,641,024 words for
15,649,920 logical words.  Power-of-two reservation would cap this run at
16,777,216 words, saving at most 22.37 MiB, roughly 4\% of the diagnostic peak.
That is useful secondary cleanup, but not a meaningful closure of the roughly
266-MiB fresh-screen gap.

\section{Decision and next action}

No memory change is promoted or sent through the expensive three-wave/gate/fuzz
path.  The measured reconstruction and dense-layout ceilings do not justify
delaying the solve-loss cohort.

Ranks 15, 16, 19, 23, and 36 are the five fresh Tactix timeouts, and all five
instances are SAT.  They now become the immediate convergence target.  Fresh
bounded accepted-Tactix/current-Kissat diagnostics will compare early
Lucky/Walk, phase and rephase state, mode switching, decisions, and learned
clause quality.  Only a shared portable owner may become a repair.  Any
acceptance claim still requires three independent repetitions, the complete
frozen B28+C5 gate, and Claude-owned fuzz.  The canceled partial Rank 1--40
refresh remains excluded from selection and inference.
