\section*{Outcome}
The Sweep original-core-unit mechanism identified in the September 7 parity
investigation is implemented, but lacked a maintained current-source proof
fixture. AM406 adds one without changing solver semantics or the public rule
registry. A four-variable saved-core row is exposed to the existing capture
and emission transaction after three host root units make its original clause
unit. The regression checks both sides of the eligibility boundary: with only
two of those root units, it captures nothing.

\section*{Proof and test evidence}
On hwulab, job \texttt{1721170} completed \texttt{0:0} in 19 seconds using two
CPUs and 8 GiB. The full Sweep unit test passed, including 86,501 small
environments and 5,344 checked claims. Its proof script verified 48
derivations through independent DRAT and LRAT checkers; six were also checked
through the strict native DSR-to-LSR-to-Trestle route. The new original-core
case is emitted in all three formats against the exact original CNF plus a
checker-only contradictory unit. The build hash, exact command and job log
are retained under \texttt{benchmarks/probes/am406-sweep-core-proof/}; the
technical report \texttt{2026-09-25-am406-sweep-original-core-proof-regression.md}
records the boundary and limitations.

Two earlier diagnostic jobs are accounted for, not hidden. The first fixture
made the original unit before environment construction, so the builder
correctly excluded it; the real mismatch occurs after import during a seed.
The second invoked the unrelated brute-force oracle with zero rounds and hit
its pre-existing nonempty-environment assertion. The final job uses the
saved-core boundary and the standard round count. No new solver defect was
established by either failed harness invocation.

\section*{Remaining gates}
This is an adapter proof regression with a posed saved core, not a replay of
the full seed-563 trajectory; the isolated historical trace remains separate.
The N4 row is implemented and proof-tested but not accepted until cumulative
correctness/performance gates and trajectory checks pass. The current Opus
fuzz and full Debug jobs continue. H2's checker contract and the
post-finalization clause-addition API decision remain open. Production online
learning still requires the previously identified inprocessing controller
and live strategy safe points. N1--N4 and Rank/B+C/WV gates are not complete,
so the requested full Tier-D 5,000-second, four-CPU, 32-GiB comparison remains
deferred.
