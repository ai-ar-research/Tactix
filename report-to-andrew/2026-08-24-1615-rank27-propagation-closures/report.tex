\section{Crash escalation and independent validation}
The initial atomic-watch prototype assumed that one doubling always leaves two
free words.  Exact batch rebuilds and defragmentation can instead leave
\code{capacity == size == 1}; one growth then produces capacity two and only one
free word.  The prototype wrote past the arena and made \code{solve_reset}
segfault after its matching reused/fresh solve summaries.

This reproduced crash triggered DEVELOPMENT\_PLAN \S8.2's mandatory bounded
Claude escalation.  Claude was granted workspace edit permission and restricted
to the two-word append, its reproducer, and focused checks.  The repair loops
until two words actually fit, retains one common-path capacity decision, and
adds an exact one-word-slice regression.  Claude reports all 73 code tests
passing; only the pre-existing opt-in documentation-size check fails.

Codex independently reviewed the patch and reran Release \code{kernel} plus
\code{solve_reset}; both pass.  Rebuilt ASan/UBSan versions of the same two
tests pass with \code{ASAN_OPTIONS=detect_leaks=0}; LeakSanitizer itself cannot
run under this execution wrapper and fails before evaluating solver memory.
The corrected candidate, not the crashing prototype, is the immutable binary
measured by job \code{1645791}.  It is correct but neutral and was fully
reverted after measurement.  No crash artifact, staged CNF, proof, or checker
derivative remains.

\section{Decision and next actions}
No solver behavior or default changes in this increment.  The accepted branch
still contains the reserved-trail representation and all earlier Sweep,
directed-bridge, BVE-admission, proof, and N1--N4 closures.  The six new
campaigns are reopening barriers: they require contradictory owner-local and
whole-prefix evidence, not merely another timing sample.

The next source comparison should price Tactix's per-literal 16-byte
\code{WatchHead} plus flat-arena offset arithmetic against Kissat's direct
begin/end vector representation.  First measure scan-entry indirection and the
population/cost of delayed relocations without changing watch order.  Any
candidate must again preserve exact logical ticks/costs, candidate order,
scheduling order, learning and pruning at common state.  A material winner then
requires exact event-trace parity, full Release and sanitizer coverage, the
complete frozen B28 plus predeclared C5 guard with checked models/proofs and zero
residue, exact-source fuzzing, and a checked two-CPU Rank-27 witness.  Frozen
reference solvers remain untouched.
