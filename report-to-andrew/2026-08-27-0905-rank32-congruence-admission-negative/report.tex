\section{Outcome}

The isolated proposal to remove Tactix's Congruence new-material admission
gate is rejected and reverted.  It is locally attractive on immutable Rank 32:
the three-wave median falls from 410.760 to 405.042 seconds.  It is not safe to
promote across the declared benchmark population: the complete B28+C5 gate has
no correctness failure or solve loss, but its PAR-2 ratio is 1.208552, above
the frozen 1.10 limit.  On the dominant witness, \code{mod4block}, runtime
increases from 19.846 to 237.094 seconds and conflicts increase from 552,580 to
5,488,183.

Source-backed tracing localizes the first candidate-owned consequence.  At an
exact common state near conflict 105525, the candidate admits one additional
Congruence round.  Its 152 candidates are in a complete recorded order, all
are classified \code{no-antecedent}, and the exact normalized cost is 456
ticks.  It learns nothing and leaves the normalized logical state unchanged.
The mandatory dense-to-sparse binary-watch replay nevertheless changes
physical propagation order before Backbone.  The first conflict-clause
difference follows at conflict 105527 and the first durable learned-clause
difference at conflict 105568.

This is not an implementation bug that can be repaired by suppressing the
pose change.  Current Kissat performs the same dense resume in Congruence, and
Tactix's replay routine deliberately implements Kissat's saved-pair order.
Deferring or skipping it would merely restore the faster non-reference
baseline behavior.  The change can be reconsidered after an earlier common-
state convergence removes this sensitivity, but it cannot be accepted now.
No fuzz campaign was run because the candidate failed the pre-fuzz gate and
was not promoted.

\section{Causal localization}

Campaign \code{20260827-0770} proves candidate and accepted baseline traces
are byte-identical through conflict 100,000: 94,555 conflict records, 91,685
learned records, and three admitted Congruence rounds.  The current-Kissat
comparison has a pre-existing Tactix/Kissat learned-state split at conflict
2515, common to both Tactix pins and unrelated to this proposal.

The wider observers \code{0771} and \code{0773} saturated their event limits
before the target and are frozen with that limitation.  Checkpoint observer
\code{0772} localizes the first candidate-only round to the next Probe: at the
first completed boundary both arms are at conflict 120,512, but candidate has
four Congruence rounds, 427,487 decisions, and 4,042,238 propagations versus
baseline's three rounds, 422,586 decisions, and 4,145,226 propagations.  Both
have zero Congruence merge and unit yield.  One terminal checkpoint task solved
SAT before its nominal observer stop and returned valid solver exit 10 while
the wrapper expected UNKNOWN exit 0; this is explicitly classified as a
harness-contract mismatch, not a solver failure.

Narrow campaigns \code{0774} and \code{0775} capture the exact boundary.  At
conflict 105525 the candidate executes \code{congruence-dense-resume}; the
baseline skips it and enters Substitute/Backbone directly.  The full candidate
stream has 152 ordered candidates, all \code{no-antecedent}, with exactly 456
ticks and no logical output.  Both trajectories remain exact through conflict
105526.  The first conflict record differs at 105527; intermittent resyncs
follow, but the first durable learned mismatch is at conflict 105568.

Kissat source \code{congruence.c} calls
\code{kissat\_resume\_sparse\_mode} after extraction, before Substitute and
Backbone.  Tactix source \code{kernel\_canonicalize\_binary\_watches} documents
and tests the corresponding global pair replay.  Baseline instead delays the
same deduplication until Vivify's dense entry, after Backbone.  Thus the
candidate's physical consequence is reference-authored.  The severe regression
comes from applying that reference schedule to a Tactix state that already has
an earlier logical difference; it is not a collection micro-optimization
opportunity at this boundary.

\section{Decision and next action}

The production source, tests, scheduling checker, and parity ledger have been
restored to the accepted predecessor.  The negative evidence remains in the
frozen manifests and living progress authority.  No candidate code remains,
so no consolidation fuzz or correctness repair workflow is applicable.

Commit and push this evidence-only closure with a clean synchronized tree.
Then rerun the immutable Rank 1--40 screen using accepted Tactix and unchanged
current Kissat: 80 independent solver tasks, one invocation per task,
\code{-c 12 --mem=48000M}, no \code{RLIMIT\_AS}, 1200-second solver limit,
and independent model/proof checking.  Select the largest verified UNSAT gap
from that fresh screen, then confirm it in three paired timing waves before
changing behavior.
