\section*{Objective and outcome}
The immediate objective was to continue the immutable Tier-D ranks 21--40
campaign in UNSAT-first order at rank 27.  The user correctly rejected the
formula-range Vivify disable as an instance-shaped policy; that repair was
completed in the preceding increment and is documented in
\artifact{report-to-andrew/2026-08-20-2357-vivify-admission-rank24/}.
This increment then compared Tactix and Kissat from rank 27's latest common
semantic state, including Vivify scheduling, candidate order, and rule
interleaving, while looking for the earliest portable divergence.

The earliest divergence turned out to precede Vivify and search.  Tactix
silently excluded AND gates above arity 64 from opening Congruence; both Kissat
and CaDiCaL ship the same option at 1,000,000.  Raising only that scope value
restores exactly 132 opening equivalence classes that Tactix was missing.  A
new arity-65 unit fixture pins the first old exclusion.

The repair passes the full required correctness and iteration guard, but rank
27 itself remains open: the repaired candidate times out UNKNOWN at
1,200.004 seconds.  Thus this is a verified semantic-parity repair, not a
Tier-D closure claim.

\section*{Localization from consumer to producer}
The retained pre-repair traces agreed through decision 69,892.  At decision
69,893, Tactix selected variable 392,211 and Kissat selected 392,214 at the
same activity score.  This made stable-heap shape the first visible search
boundary.  The consumer was then ruled out:

\begin{itemize}
\item conflict 1532, its bumped-variable stream, and its top-32 heap were exact;
\item all 35,887 heap reinsertions through conflict 1532 matched in order;
\item Tactix and Kissat use the same strict activity comparison, equal-score
  stopping rule, and left-child preference.
\end{itemize}

The opening producer state was already different.  Kissat's Congruence merged
58,722 variables: 55,658 through AND gates, 2,995 through XOR gates, and 69
through paired binary equivalences.  Tactix merged 58,590: 55,526 AND, 2,995
XOR, and 69 binary.  The entire 132-variable population difference was
therefore in AND processing, and it explained the later heap-shape tie without
requiring a downstream tie breaker.

Diagnostic Slurm job \code{1636967} measured Tactix's gate-kind funnel.  Job
\code{1636968} instrumented the unchanged Kissat semantics and showed that
ITE-to-AND conversions contributed zero of Kissat's opening AND merges.  Job
\code{1636970} dumped both final representative partitions; they differed on
exactly 132 variables.  The first witness was
\code{93231 == -93230} in Kissat while Tactix left 93231 singleton.

The defining clauses form matching 72-input gates.  Selected-gate job
\code{1636972} showed that Tactix never created the high-fan-in gate: its
\code{CONG\_ARITY\_LIMIT = 64} test rejected the base clause before the
worklist.  This matched correction C7 in
\artifact{docs/scheduling-parity-ledger.md}, which had already identified the
64-versus-1,000,000 value gap and named the exact reference-parity repair.

\section*{Implementation decision}
The production change is one scope value in
\artifact{src/rules/congruence/congruence.cpp}: AND arity 64 becomes
1,000,000.  Tactix retains its separately documented mid-round work budget as
the effort governor.  The gate representation, extraction order, union-find,
proof construction, scheduling, and public rule surface do not change.

\artifact{tests/test_congruence.cpp} now builds two congruent 65-input AND
gates and requires their outputs to merge.  Arity 65 is deliberately the
first old exclusion.  The fixture is independent of rank 27 and would catch a
future silent narrowing of the reference-wide opportunity.

\section*{Limitations, risks, and next action}
The repaired opening Congruence partition now matches Kissat in the dimension
that caused the previous search split, but no claim is made that all later
semantic state is exact.  The old decision-69,893 boundary depended on the
132-variable population gap and is obsolete for the repaired source.  Rank 27
also shows that restoring a missing proof opportunity need not improve the
overall search trajectory by itself.

The next concrete action is to build a fresh semantic-trace pin and compare it
with the retained diagnostic Kissat build beginning after the corrected
58,722-merge opening Congruence/Substitute state.  The first new portable
semantic, scheduling, or representation divergence should be localized before
any further edit.  Any new candidate again owes the full Tier-B plus
predeclared Tier-C guard before another rank-27 witness.
