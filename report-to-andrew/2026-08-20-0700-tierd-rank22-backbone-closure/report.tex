\section*{Implementation decisions}
The repair follows Kissat's chained binary-UIP batch rather than tuning a
budget or adding a witness-specific gate.

\begin{itemize}
\item Chained binary implications are assigned at the current accumulated
assumption level.  A derived unit remains speculative through the rest of its
round; the complete round suffix is unwound by trail position before the unit
batch is replayed at root.
\item Later computations exclude variables already fixed at root, matching
Kissat's \code{ACTIVE\_VAR} representation while retaining Tactix's public
variable identities and complete one-literal \code{backbone\_try} surface.
\item The binary watch cursor consumes the first fetched large-watch word on
an early stop.  Conversely, a conflicting binary returns before charging that
literal's scan or private ticks, matching the reference control flow exactly.
\item Post-round general root propagation is outside Backbone's private meter.
Startup propagation, both Lucky trials, sparse root collection, and initial
Probe are likewise excluded from \code{search\_ticks}; initial Probe leaves the
search-time Probe anchor at zero as in Kissat.
\item Binary-only propagation records the trail prefix whose large watches it
skipped.  Every full propagation entry settles that debt, and verdicts and
guards use the resulting common fixpoint predicate.
\end{itemize}

These changes are documented in \artifact{docs/calculus.md},
\artifact{docs/implementation-notes.md}, and the closed
\code{SP-BACKBONE-3} row of
\artifact{docs/scheduling-parity-ledger.md}.  Focused regressions cover the
speculative-unit lifecycle, current-level propagation, root replay and its
meter boundary, the first large watch word, the uncharged binary-conflict
scan, large-clause root closure, and native-LRAT output.

\section*{Mandatory soundness escalation}
The first quick random-policy corpus replay, Slurm job \code{1636407}, found
two real failures: corpus 004 returned a SAT model falsifying clause 199, and
corpus 023 returned an UNSAT proof rejected by \code{lrat-check}.  This fired
the development plan's mandatory Claude escalation.  The first static repair
passed focused tests but not either exact case in Slurm job \code{1636634}; it
was therefore rejected rather than promoted.

Claude then reproduced both cases with their recorded schedules and real
checkers.  Their common cause was a sessionless Sweep substitution: an early
return left the transaction's retiring-literal list empty, so the physical
rewrite could delete one direction of a binary while retaining the other.
That half-watched clause could lose its proof identity and poison LRAT, or a
whole binary could disappear and permit a wrong model.  The repaired session
records membership independently of the optional dense ledger, with a focused
mirror-and-identity regression and a kernel invariant for unnamed binary
reasons.

Validation fuzzing then found corpus 026: Sweep's RUP admission replay was a
no-op outside native LRAT, so DRAT and proofless runs could admit lemmas that
unit propagation could not rederive.  An explicit \code{RupReplay} purpose now
separates Congruence's chain-only use from the format-independent admission
decision shared by Sweep and HyperBinaryResolution.  The minimized DRAT case
and a proof-format parity regression pin the repair; the complete diagnosis is
\artifact{benchmarks/reports/2026-08-20-sweep-drat-admission-gate.md}.

The final exact model, native-LRAT, and DRAT reproducers pass.  Four complete
26-entry corpus configurations report zero failures.  Release passes 72/72,
Trace 71/71, ASAN-O3 72/72, and Debug 71/71 tests; Debug's multi-hour scripted
demo is covered by the other three configurations.  Additional random-policy
fuzzing covers 1,200 DRAT and 800 native-LRAT cases with zero failures and
direct Sweep and HyperBinaryResolution reachability.  Final Slurm job
\code{1636794} exercises the immutable Debug pin \code{8662f1f1...} on 10,000
more random-policy cases: zero failures, 8,771 model/proof/differential-checked
verdicts, and direct Backbone, Sweep, and HyperBinaryResolution reachability.
Leak detection remains disabled only for the known LeakSanitizer-under-ptrace
environment limitation.
