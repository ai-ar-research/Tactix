\section*{Outcome}
The frozen AM402 source audit gives a qualified yes to the missing-proof-rules question. The verified SC26 LymphoSAT bundle has listed specialized solvers whose UNSAT paths emit general pseudo-Boolean (PB) polynomial arithmetic, redundancy and subproof derivations. Its composite verifies a returned UNSAT certificate with VeriPB before publishing it. The pinned Satsuma source also has PB dominance/arithmetic output. Tactix can emit PB arithmetic in Lucky 19/20, but only for complete original-input terminal refutations at its startup proof boundary. It has no general live PB derivation action or event interface. The separate clausal vocabulary---RUP, RAT, finite substitution redundancy and deletion---appears represented; this source pass did not establish a missing clausal primitive.

\section*{Evidence and interpretation}
The LymphoSAT \code{order.json} and generated matcher registry include equivalence-chain, XOR-chain, Tseitin-formula and random-XOR solvers. Their source emits \code{pol} addition/scaling/division, \code{red} constraints and/or PB subproofs; the AM402 technical report gives exact source locations. A literal-source count finds \code{pol} in at least 15 family producers, \code{red} in at least seven and \code{pbc} in at least three; these are lower bounds, not measured competition usage. Tactix's Lucky parity and count emitters use some of the same PB commands, so the gap is general applicability and proof-stream integration, not total absence of the arithmetic syntax.

This is a proof-calculus and proof-efficiency design gap, not automatically an N1 failure for the currently declared action catalog. Nor does PB dominance syntax alone prove a missing semantic symmetry action: Tactix's X7 can carry finite clausal substitution witnesses. A general fix would require a typed PB derivation interface, legality and resource bounds, original-CNF proof checking, and a deliberate choice about the present startup-PBP versus ordinary-DSR boundary. Splicing PB commands into a published DSR stream would not satisfy the current N2 contract. The remaining CaDiCaL/Kissat producer and public reachability audits still need to be completed.
