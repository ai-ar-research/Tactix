\section*{Verified evidence and limits}
Tier A: both arms solved 199/200 with 117 independently checked UNSAT
proofs each, and matching paired verdicts and proof sizes. Tier B: both
arms solved 28/28, with every proof and model checked and matching paired
verdicts/proof sizes. At 19:20 UTC, Tier C array \texttt{1723299} held
55/60 result records: 27 complete pairs were checker-clean and matched in
verdict and proof size; five heavy tasks remained live, and dependent
aggregate \texttt{1723300} had not run. The current-source Debug CTest job
\texttt{1723391} remained queued. These are not final Tier-C or N4 results.

The H2 current-source 21-variable proof is byte-identical to the historical
parent. Frozen no-option \texttt{dsr-trim} rejects it backward and forward;
the same proof verifies with \texttt{--delete-implied-units} through frozen
Trestle. Checker source review explains the mismatch, but the frozen-binary
replay is the authority. Neither a fixed deletion order nor publishing all
root units is a general repair. The option changes proof-state semantics and
needs an explicit contract decision plus broad positive and negative audits;
the no-option route remains canonical meanwhile. Public action tests reach
all 29 registry entries, but that is not proof of every legal N1 boundary.

\section*{Tier D and next work}
The first-winner authority contains 1,166 unique SC24--26 instances. The
requested per-task contract is four CPUs, 32 GiB, 5,000 solver seconds and
45,000 checker seconds on \texttt{cpu-dense-hwulab-q}. Its old 23-task throttle
was a scratch-management measure; plan \S8.1 now requires full-width,
unthrottled arrays with anonymous node-local scratch. The dedicated
\texttt{tactix\_tierd.py} currently forces \texttt{--drat}. An existing
\texttt{runtime\_task.py} route already supports automatic PBP, witnessed
DSR, and witness-free DSR with original-CNF checking; adapt and pin that
route before the post-goal comparison. Finish Tier C and current Debug/fuzz
validation, resolve H2 generally, then close the retained coverage,
performance, rule-parity and N1--N4 gates. The online-learning architecture
assessment in the prior AM430 report remains unchanged: production learning
needs shared slot-control, owned switchable sub-policies, and live policy
session ownership.
