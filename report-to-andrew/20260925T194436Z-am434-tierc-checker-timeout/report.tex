\section*{Outcome}
The matched AM427 Sweep Tier-C array is terminal but \emph{not} a clean
correctness or performance gate. Both arms solved 29/30 with independently
checked models or proofs; on the same remaining AES instance, each emitted
UNSAT but its native DSR checker exhausted the campaign's 4,800-second
budget. The dependent aggregate deliberately failed with a
\texttt{CORRECTNESS-BUG} resource-failure marker. This is a timeout, not a
reported proof rejection, and neither permits an N2 acceptance claim.
The detailed evidence is
\href{../../benchmarks/reports/2026-09-25-am434-sweep-tier-c-checker-timeout.md}{AM434}.

\section*{Exact evidence}
All 60 task records are present and all 30 candidate--parent verdicts and
proof sizes match: 15 SAT and 15 UNSAT verdicts per arm. Fifteen SAT models
and fourteen UNSAT proofs per arm passed their independent original-CNF
checks. On \texttt{aes\_decry\_2\_rounds}, the two 238,948,969-byte DSR
proofs each reached the 4,800-second checker ceiling after roughly
100-second solves. The benchmark cleaned those proof artifacts, so its
equal-size records alone did not prove byte identity. Its 29/30 PAR-2
headline is unscorable as a complete gate; the small apparent paired
runtime gain is single-case-sensitive and does not establish N4.

A separate, immutable two-task diagnostic array, \texttt{1723443}, now
repeats only this original input and both exact solver binary hashes with
the frozen no-option DSR/Trestle checkers. It preserves the Tier-C
1,200-second solve cap, eight CPUs and 32 GiB, and extends only the checker
allowance to 45,000 seconds. Both provisional solver records again report
UNSAT and now show identical full proof SHA-256 values
(\texttt{6a04b015\ldots db4a62}; full hash in AM434).
That is certificate identity, \emph{not} verification: both checker verdicts
remain pending. The diagnostic uses anonymous node-local scratch and has
not changed the frozen Tier-C run.

\section*{Next actions and separate blockers}

failure as a resource-limit result and design a matched, independently
checked successor gate; if it rejects or exhausts 45,000 seconds, diagnose
the exact stage before further performance work. The current-source full
Debug job \texttt{1723391} is running, while affected Claude-owned fuzz
